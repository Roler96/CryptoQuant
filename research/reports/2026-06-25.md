## Anti-Patterns Discovered

### 2026-06-25 Loop 10: KAMA on 4h — Adaptive Smoothing Kills Signal Count
**Problem:** KamaTrend produced 12 trades on both BTC 4h and ETH 4h. KAMA's adaptive smoothing (efficiency ratio → dynamic smoothing constant) makes it even slower than a fixed EMA on higher timeframes.
**Root cause:** KAMA was designed for daily charts. On 4h, the efficiency ratio takes weeks to change direction, and crossovers become extremely rare (12/year vs 68-71 on 1h).
**Lesson:** Adaptive/efficiency-based indicators should be restricted to 1h or lower timeframes. For 4h+, prefer fixed-parameter indicators or double signal frequency by shortening lookback.

### 2026-06-25 Loop 10: Force Index — Volume-Weighted Momentum Works on Crypto
**Contrary to Loop 6 CLV finding:** Force Index (which incorporates volume into momentum) worked well on BTC 1h (81 trades, Sharpe=2.40) and ETH 1h (79 trades, Sharpe=1.34) with full OOS validation on ETH. This is notable because Loop 6 found CLV-based entries failed on crypto.
**Key difference:** Force Index uses volume×price_change rather than bar-position metrics. Volume weighting filters noise without adding AND conditions — the volume term naturally suppresses weak bars without creating false negatives.
**Lesson:** Volume-weighted momentum (Force Index) is viable on crypto 1h. Position-based metrics (CLV) are not. The distinction matters: volume multiplies signal strength, CLV gates entry — the former preserves signal count, the latter kills it.

### 2026-06-25 Loop 10: 4h Trade Scarcity — Now 5th Instance
This is the 5th consecutive loop where 4h timeframes fail purely on insufficient trades (≤15 trades/year). Across Loops 5-10: RangeExpansionBreakout 4h (30-80), InsideBarBreakout 4h (66-70), MacdAdxTrend 4h (65-72), KamaTrend 4h (12), ForceIndexTrend 4h (11-14). Only breakout-based strategies (channel/inside-bar) generate enough 4h trades.
**Lesson:** For 4h timeframes, use only breakout-based entries (1-bar or 20-bar channel). Avoid all momentum/oscillator/crossover/acceleration entries on 4h — they will produce <30 trades in a 365-day window.
