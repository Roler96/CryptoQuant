## Key Findings

1. **1h breakout strategies dominate:** All 4 1h combos passed. Both strategies (BB+Volume and Channel+RSI) perform well across BTC and ETH on 1h, with Sharpe 2.05-3.40.

2. **4h breakout strategies are fragile:** 3/4 4h combos failed. Even the one that passed (ChannelBreakoutRSI BTC 4h) barely cleared the trade threshold (31). Breakout patterns are too sparse on 4h bars in a 365-day window. Need either >2 years of data or wider parameters.

3. **Volume as confirmation filter works:** BBandBreakoutVolume uses volume > SMA(volume, 20) as the confirmation gate — all 1h combos passed with strong OOS Sharpe. Volume filters add robustness without being overly restrictive (unlike the AND-gate anti-patterns from previous loops).

4. **RSI as confirmation filter works:** ChannelBreakoutRSI uses RSI > 50 / < 50 — effective simple filter. Adding more constraints (e.g., ADX threshold from Loop 2's Keltner+ADX) would likely push this below the trade threshold.

5. **OOS Sharpe > IS Sharpe on BTC — regime tailwind:** Both strategies show OOS Sharpe > IS Sharpe on BTC pairs, suggesting the recent 4-month OOS period was more favorable to trend-following breakouts than the prior 8 months.
