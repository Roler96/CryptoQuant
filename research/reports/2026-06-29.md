### 4h OOS: Universal Trade Count Failure

**Problem:** ALL 4 4h combos failed OOS due to trade count < 30 in the OOS window. CMO BTC 4h: 23 OOS trades. CMO ETH 4h: 22 OOS trades. VWAP BTC 4h: 23 OOS trades. VWAP ETH 4h: 24 OOS trades. This is the 6th consecutive loop where 4h fails OOS purely on sample size.

**Root cause:** The OOS window (last ~4 months = ~730 1h bars but only ~180 4h bars) is too short to generate 30+ trades on 4h. Even strategies with 72+ full-sample trades produce only 22-24 in the OOS slice.

**Lesson:** 4h OOS validation is structurally impossible in a 365-day backtest with 70/30 IS/OOS split. The OOS window contains only 180 bars — insufficient for any non-breakout strategy to generate 30 trades. Either: (1) extend lookback to 2 years for 4h, (2) use 80/20 split, or (3) skip OOS validation for 4h and rely on IS Sharpe + MaxDD alone.

**Lesson:** ETH OOS robustness requires either (a) self-resetting indicators with no lookback (VWAP), or (b) volume-weighted signal multipliers (Force Index). Fixed-lookback oscillators will always overfit ETH's IS period and fail OOS. The ETH curse is not about ETH — it's about lookback window overfitting.
