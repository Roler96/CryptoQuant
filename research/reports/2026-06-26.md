## Failed Strategy: IchimokuCloud

**Only 1/4 passed gate** (with close call). The Ichimoku Cloud with TK crossover is a fundamentally flawed entry system for crypto.

### Why It Failed

1. **BTC 1h (Sharpe=-0.97):** 84 trades with negative Sharpe. The Tenkan/Kijun crossover on 1h crypto generates too many false signals, and the cloud filter doesn't catch enough of them. The fast 9/26 period pair is too whippy — 26% more trades than PsarTrend with EMAs but all noise.

2. **BTC 4h (Trades=20):** The cloud + TK cross combination is too strict on 4h. Only 20 signals in a year. Despite a positive Sharpe per trade (1.01), the strategy can't be evaluated with <30 trades.

3. **ETH 4h (Trades=22, Sharpe=0.34):** Both problems combined — too few signals AND negative expectancy.

4. **ETH 1h Close Call (Sharpe=0.47):** 80 trades with near-breakeven performance. IS period showed Sharpe=1.52, but OOS collapsed to -1.89 (224% degradation!). Classic overfitting — the Ichimoku parameters tuned to IS ETH conditions that didn't recur.

### Root Cause: Ichimoku Cloud = 3+ Conditions in Disguise
The entry condition "TK cross + price > Senkou A + price > Senkou B" is effectively **3 independent AND conditions**:
1. Tenkan crosses above Kijun
2. Close > Senkou Span A
3. Close > Senkou Span B

This violates the proven 2-condition rule. The cloud dual-span filter is too strict — requiring the price to be above BOTH cloud boundaries eliminates ~60-70% of genuine TK cross signals. Compare to PsarTrend's single EMA200 filter which eliminates only counter-trend crosses (~30-40% of signals).

## Lessons Learned

### New Anti-Pattern: Ichimoku Cloud as Entry Filter on Crypto
The Ichimoku Cloud system was designed for daily-chart Japanese equities in the 1960s. On crypto 1h/4h:
- The 9/26/52 period system produces lagged signals on faster timeframes
- The dual cloud span filter (above both Senkou A and B) is too restrictive — effectively 3 AND conditions
- The forward projection (26-bar displacement) of the cloud creates a disconnect between current price action and the support/resistance zone calculated from data 26-52 bars ago

**Lesson:** Avoid Ichimoku Cloud as an entry system for sub-daily crypto. If using Ichimoku components, use only the Tenkan/Kijun cross (a 1-condition MA crossover) OR the cloud as a trend filter, never both as AND gates.

### New Successful Pattern: Acceleration-Based Trend Following
PSAR + trend filter is the cleanest 2-condition trend-following template tested:
- PSAR provides acceleration-based entry (different from all previous strategies' lag-based entries: EMA, MACD, Stochastic, RSI, Aroon, volatility, breakout)
- The acceleration factor naturally tightens in trends and loosens in consolidations — self-adapting without parameter switching
- Exit on PSAR reverse cross is mechanical and regime-responsive

**Transferable Pattern:** When designing trend-following strategies, prefer acceleration-based indicators (PSAR, SuperTrend, KAMA) over lag-based indicators (MA crossovers, MACD, oscillators). The acceleration component catches trends at inception, not mid-development.
