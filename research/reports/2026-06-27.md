## Key Findings

### 1. STC (Schaff Trend Cycle) — Viable BTC-Only Trend Oscillator
STC combines MACD(23,50) into a Stochastic %K, producing faster turning points than MACD alone. Entry: STC > 25 (long) / < 75 (short) + EMA200 filter. Exactly 2 conditions. Generated 158 trades on BTC 1h — fast enough to avoid the signal-sparsity anti-pattern. Works on both 1h and 4h BTC.

**Transferable Pattern:** Dual-smoothed composite oscillators (STC = Stochastic(MACD)) can overcome the 4h trade-scarcity problem because the inner MACD provides trend direction while the outer Stochastic normalization accelerates turning points. The 0-100 normalization is key — it works universally across timeframes without threshold tuning.

### 2. TMF Zero-Cross — Too Slow for Crypto
TMF (Twiggs Money Flow) applies Wilder EMA smoothing to volume-weighted money flow. The zero-cross condition requires TMF to change sign — a rare event on sub-daily crypto timeframes. Even on 1h (8760 bars), only 1 zero-cross occurred in 365 days. This is worse than KAMA (12 trades), PSAR (20-22 trades), and other acceleration-based indicators.

**Root cause:** Wilder EMA (α=1/N) smooths aggressively. With tmf_period=21, TMF takes weeks to reverse direction. Money flow in crypto is noisy but directionally persistent — it rarely cleanly crosses zero within a 365-day window.

### 3. ETH Remains Hostile to Oscillator/Momentum Strategies
STCTrend showed perfect BTC/ETH bifurcation: Sharpe 1.37-1.76 on BTC, Sharpe 0.03-0.30 on ETH. This is the consistent pattern across 12 loops: oscillator-based trend strategies produce positive Sharpe on BTC but near-zero on ETH. The ETH OOS window (Feb-Jun 2026) continues to systematically degrade oscillator-family strategies.
