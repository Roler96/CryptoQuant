## Anti-Patterns Identified

### Elder Ray on ETH — Complete Failure (9th ETH OOS Instance)

Elder Ray Bull/Bear Power uses bar extremes (high/low) vs EMA as the signal. On ETH, this produces severely negative results: Sharpe -1.34 on 1h (179 trades) and 0.02 on 4h (35 trades). The indicator's dependence on high/low extremes makes it vulnerable to ETH's wick-heavy microstructure. False bull/bear power extremes trigger entries on noise.

- **Lesson:** Bar-extreme-based indicators (Elder Ray, High-Minus-Low range metrics) are unreliable on ETH. ETH's wick frequency and fragmented liquidity produce false extreme readings that don't represent genuine directional pressure.

### Elder Ray on 4h — Trade Scarcity (6th Instance)

BTC 4h barely missed gate at 28 trades (Sharpe 1.94, excellent). ETH 4h scraped past at 35 trades but Sharpe 0.02. Bar-extreme indicators on 4h face the same sample-size problem as all non-breakout indicators — only 2190 bars/year vs 8760 on 1h, and each signal requires a bar extreme to cross an EMA, which is rarer than a close crossing.

- **Lesson:** Bar-extreme-based indicators (Elder Ray, Donchian extremes, Keltner pierce) should target 1h for sufficient signal density.
