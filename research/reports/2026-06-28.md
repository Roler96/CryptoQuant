## New Anti-Patterns

### MamaFama: Adaptive EMA on 4h/ETH (Confirms Loop 10 KAMA Finding)

MamaFamaTrend (adaptive EMA crossover based on Ehlers MAMA/FAMA) produced only 13-18 trades on 4h and net-negative Sharpe on ETH 1h (-0.12). This confirms the Loop 10 finding: adaptive/efficiency-based indicators (KAMA, MAMA/FAMA, adaptive EMA, VIDYA) compound the 4h trade-scarcity problem. The adaptive smoothing self-adjusts to longer periods in noisy markets, reducing crossover frequency below the 30-trade threshold.

**Lesson:** Adaptive smoothing indicators are 1h-only. For 4h, use fixed-parameter indicators or breakout-based entries.

### RMI: Weakest Valid Oscillator

RMI (Relative Momentum Index) uses up/down momentum days instead of up/down closes (RSI). It passes BTC 1h gate with the lowest Sharpe of any valid strategy (0.56). All other combos failed: BTC 4h (16 trades), ETH 1h (Sharpe=0.32), ETH 4h (20 trades). RMI's momentum-basis change does not improve signal quality — the standard RSI period (14) already captures momentum through close changes.

**Lesson:** Raw momentum oscillators (RSI variants, RMI, MOM) are the weakest of the validated oscillator families. For momentum-based trend following, prefer CCI (deviation measurement) or Williams %R (raw position) which outperform RSI variants on both Sharpe and trade count.

### Cumulative Volume Family Now 14/16 (87.5%)

PVT joins OBV and Force Index as the 3rd cumulative-volume family member with strong performance. The proportional weighting (volume × %∆price) generates 4-5× more signals than binary OBV while preserving the cumulative line's noise-smoothing. PVT is the best-in-class cumulative volume indicator.
