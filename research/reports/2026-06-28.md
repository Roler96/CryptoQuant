## New Anti-Patterns

### 2026-06-28 Loop 15: ROC/Price-Momentum ETH 1h OOS Catastrophe — 10th Documented Instance

**Problem:** ROCTrend ETH 1h: IS Sharpe=1.23 → OOS Sharpe=0.10 (91.9% degradation). This is the 10th documented catastrophic ETH OOS failure across 8 loops, and the 3rd raw-price-momentum indicator to fail (following CLV in Loop 6 and CMF in Loop 14).

**Root cause:** ROC = close[t] - close[t-N] — raw price change over a lookback window. On ETH 1h, the OOS period's choppy mean-reverting behavior generates ROC crossovers that don't sustain beyond 2-3 bars. Unlike RSI (which measures position WITHIN recent range, 0-100), ROC is sensitive to absolute price magnitude and doesn't self-calibrate to volatility regimes.

**Lesson:** Raw-price-momentum indicators (ROC, raw return, price-change threshold) are MORE fragile on ETH than self-normalizing indicators (RSI, %B, Stochastic, CMO). The difference: normalized indicators measure relative position; raw indicators measure absolute change. ETH's volatile microstructure produces large absolute ROC movements that don't represent genuine trend shifts. Prefer normalized oscillators (RSI, %B, CMO) over raw-price indicators (ROC, CLV, Elder Ray, Aroon) for ETH.

**Problem:** All 4 4h combos fail OOS on trade count (10-20 OOS trades), despite main-gate passes. This includes RSIExpansionTrend (14-20 OOS trades) and ROCTrend (10-12 OOS trades) on both BTC and ETH.

**Root cause:** The 30% OOS split extracts 657 bars from a 2190-bar 4h window. Even strategies with 34-78 full-sample trades drop to 10-20 in OOS because trades are distributed across the full year, not concentrated in the last 30%. The 30-trade OOS gate is equivalent to requiring ~100 full-sample trades (30/0.3 = 100).

**Lesson:** The OOS trade-count gate (≥30) on 4h is too strict for the 30% split. A strategy needs 80-100+ full-sample trades to have a reasonable chance of landing 30 in the OOS period. With 2190 bars/year, only breakout-based strategies (1-bar inside-bar, %B threshold, Dual Thrust) can reach this density. Consider either: (a) accepting main-gate-only validation for 4h with OOS as a directional indicator (positive OOS Sharpe even with <30 trades is meaningful), or (b) using a 50/50 split for 4h to get more OOS bars.
