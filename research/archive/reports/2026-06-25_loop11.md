## Anti-Patterns Discovered

### 2026-06-25 Loop 11: Acceleration Indicators on 4h — Confirmed 3rd Instance
**Problem:** SuperTrendTrend produced only 28 trades on both BTC 4h and ETH 4h. This joins PSAR (Loop 9, 20-22 trades on 4h) and KAMA (Loop 10, 12 trades on 4h) as the 3rd acceleration/adaptive indicator to fail 4h on trade count.
**Root cause:** Acceleration-based indicators (SuperTrend, PSAR, KAMA) all tighten/loosen their signal bands based on market conditions. On 4h, bar frequency is 4× lower than 1h, and the band-tightening logic extends signal waits proportionally — double the timeframe width, half the signals. The ATR period (10 for SuperTrend) = 40 hours on 4h before first signal, then each recross takes additional 30-50 hours.
**Lesson:** Acceleration-based and adaptive indicators should be restricted to 1h or lower. For 4h trend following, use fixed-parameter breakouts (channel breach, %B threshold) where signal frequency isn't throttled by internal adaptation logic.

**Problem:** BBPercentBVolatility ETH 4h: IS Sharpe=1.07 → OOS Sharpe=0.03 (97.2% degradation). This is the 8th documented catastrophic ETH OOS failure across 6 loops.
**Root cause:** The OOS period (Feb-Jun 2026) continues to systematically degrade all breakout and trend-following strategies on ETH 4h. The regime is structurally mean-reverting — BB %B pushes to 0.8+ but price reverts to midline rather than trending. The strategy fires correctly on technical signals but the market behavior during the OOS window doesn't cooperate.
**Lesson:** Any ETH 4h combo passing the main gate should be viewed as IS-period-dependent until proven otherwise across multiple disjoint time windows. The pattern is now too consistent to ignore — the Feb-Jun 2026 ETH 4h regime is hostile to trend-following and breakout strategies.

### 2026-06-25 Loop 11: BB %B on 4h — The Fix for 4h Trade Scarcity
**Contrary to 5 previous loops:** BBPercentBVolatility generates 36-50 trades on 4h, where 5 consecutive loops of momentum/oscillator/crossover strategies produced 5-15 trades on 4h. **Lesson:** For 4h viability, use normalized indicators (%B, Stochastic, RSI) or price-action breakouts (channel breach, inside bar). Avoid smoothed, adaptive, or cross-based indicators — the smoothing itself is the signal killer on low-frequency bars.
