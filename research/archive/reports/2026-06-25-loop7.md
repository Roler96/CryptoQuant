## Key Findings

### 1. Stochastic Outperforms Aroon — But Both Are Viable
Stochastic is more responsive to short-term momentum shifts than RSI or Aroon. On BTC 1h, it achieved Sharpe=2.35 with OOS=2.76 (no degradation) vs Aroon's Sharpe=1.49 with OOS=1.68. Stochastic's edge: it measures where price sits within range (percentage), making it inherently normalized — works across volatility regimes without parameter adjustment.

### 2. The BTC/ETH Divergence Is Systematic
Both strategies work on BTC (4/4 combos passed), and both fail on ETH 1h (2/2 failed). This is not strategy-specific — it's an ETH effect. The OOS period (Feb-Jun 2026) shows ETH in a choppy, range-bound microstructure that breaks momentum-based entries regardless of indicator choice. BTC trended during the same period.

### 3. OOS Catastrophe on ETH — Now 5th and 6th Instances
This loop adds 2 more ETH OOS failures to the growing pattern:
- StochRSITrend ETH 1h: IS=1.18 → OOS=-0.93 (179% degradation)
