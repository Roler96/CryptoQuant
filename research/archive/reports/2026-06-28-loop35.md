## Anti-Patterns Discovered (Loop 35)

### Laguerre RSI: Reduced Lag ≠ Better Signal Quality for Trend Following

**Problem:** Laguerre RSI's 4-pole gamma filter (gamma=0.5) replaces Wilder's smoothing with faster response — but on crypto 1h, the extra signals are noise, not edge. Standard RSI (RSIExpansionTrend, Loop 15) achieved Sharpe 4.39 on BTC 1h with the identical ATR expansion filter. Laguerre RSI produced Sharpe=-0.11 on the same combo.

**Root cause:** Wilder's smoothing in standard RSI acts as an implicit noise filter — it doesn't respond to single-bar reversals. Laguerre RSI's faster response produces crossovers on noise bars that standard RSI ignores. For trend-following entries (RSI>50 = long), fewer-but-better signals strictly dominate more-but-worse signals.

**Lesson:** Laguerre RSI is designed for mean reversion (RSI<30 oversold, RSI>70 overbought) where timeliness matters. For trend following (RSI>50 = directional bias), standard RSI's smoothing is a feature, not a bug. Match indicator design to strategy type: fast-response indicators for mean reversion, smoothed indicators for trend following.
