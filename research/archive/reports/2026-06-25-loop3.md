## Key Observations

### TrendPullbackRSI — The Breakthrough Strategy

TrendPullbackRSI achieves what every prior strategy failed to do: 4/4 across BTC/ETH + 1h/4h. The key ingredients:

1. **EMA(200) trend filter** — directly addresses the anti-pattern from Loop 1 (RSIBBMeanReversion failed because it didn't filter for trend direction). Never trades against the prevailing trend.

2. **Moderate RSI thresholds (40-50 long, 50-60 short)** — these are "pullback" levels, not "reversal" levels. The strategy buys strength during temporary weakness rather than betting on reversals.

3. **RSI direction confirmation** (rising/falling) — adds a second layer of timing precision, reducing false entries.

4. **Tight, symmetrical exits** — ATR-based SL/TP with 1:2 risk-reward ratio + 48-bar time limit. Profits are taken quickly, losses are cut quickly. This explains the 98% win rate — the exit logic is more important than the entry logic.

### AdaptiveStopMomentum — Right Idea, Wrong Entry

The adaptive trailing stop exit mechanism is sound (MaxDD never exceeded 5.4%), but the entry condition is too weak. A simple 2% price change threshold generates noise-level signals that the trailing stop can't salvage. The 37% win rate confirms entries are random. Adding an EMA trend filter + RSI confirmation (a la TrendPullbackRSI) could transform this into a viable strategy.

### ⚠️ Caution: Extreme Metrics Warrant Sanity Check

TrendPullbackRSI's Sharpe ratios (14-18) and win rates (98-99%) are in the "too good to be true" range. While OOS consistency (Sharpe 12-17) provides some validation, these numbers suggest:

1. **Commission model may be too generous** — 5bps round-trip with 3033 trades/year means commission cost = 3033 × 0.001 = 303% of initial capital. If the backtester isn't compounding commissions correctly, reported Sharpe could be inflated.

2. **Slippage assumptions matter** — at 5bps slippage, the strategy nets 1.82% per win and loses -2.06% per loss. Any increase in slippage would eat the thin edge.

3. **Recommendation:** Before deploying, run a commission sensitivity sweep (1bps→20bps) and a slippage stress test to determine the breakeven point.
