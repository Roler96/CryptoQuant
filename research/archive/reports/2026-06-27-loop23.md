## Detailed Analysis

### TSITrend: Where Did It Go Wrong?

TSI (True Strength Index) uses double-exponential smoothing: `EMA(EMA(Δp, short), long) / EMA(EMA(|Δp|, short), long)`. The resulting signal is extremely stable — it changes direction slowly and produces strong momentum readings.

Combined with EMA200 trend filter (enter only when close > EMA200), the strategy selects for periods of strong directional momentum. On crypto's highly trending 2025-2026 data, this captures nearly every sustained move.

**Possible root causes for the impossible results:**
1. **Look-ahead bias in indicator computation**: TSI may use `close[t]` to compute `signal[t]`, and the backtest engine enters at `close[t]` instead of `open[t+1]`. Even though the bias check passed on 3/4 combos, the flag on BTC 4h suggests a systematic issue.
2. **Double smoothing artifact**: Both TSI (2 EMA layers) and TrendPullbackRSI from Loop 3 (RSI + EMA) produce 97-99% win rates. Double smoothing on trending data creates signals that appear prescient because they're effectively averaging future information through the smoothing window.
3. **EMA200 trend filter as implicit look-ahead**: Entering only when close > EMA200 selects trades during uptrends. If the backtest engine checks close[t] > EMA200[t] and enters at close[t], it's using the same bar's information for both confirmation and entry.

## New Anti-Patterns

### 1. TSI / Double-Smoothed Indicators Produce Mathematically Impossible Results

TSITrend joins TrendPullbackRSI (Loop 3) as the 2nd strategy to produce 97-99% win rates and Sharpe >12. Both use double smoothing: TSI's internal double EMA for TSITrend, RSI + EMA for TrendPullbackRSI. The root cause appears to be an interaction between double smoothing and the EMA200 trend filter — the combination creates signals that, even with correct next-bar-open entry, pull massive unrealized gains from strongly trending crypto data.

**Lesson:** Indicators with ≥2 layers of exponential smoothing (TSI, double EMA, TRIX, TEMA) + EMA trend filter produce suspiciously high win rates on crypto. Treat any Sharpe >5.0 from such strategies as prima facie evidence of a bug. Do not include in deployment candidates without manual trade-by-trade audit.

### 2. BB Squeeze on ETH — Complete Trade Scarcity (1-4 Trades)

BBSqueezeBreakout produced 4 trades on ETH 1h and 1 trade on ETH 4h. The squeeze-threshold mechanism (BB width at 125-bar minimum) requires 125 consecutive bars where BB width is contracting before a squeeze is detected. ETH's persistent 24/7 volatility means BB width rarely contracts to a multi-month minimum — the bands stay wide, and squeezes almost never trigger.

**Lesson:** BB squeeze detection is BTC-only. ETH's volatility structure prevents the prolonged low-volatility consolidation periods that squeeze patterns require. This joins the growing list of strategies that work on BTC but fail catastrophically on ETH (TRIX Loop 22, Aroon Loop 7, CLV Loop 6).

### 3. BB Squeeze 4h — Confirmed Trade Scarcity (1-29 Trades)

BBSqueezeBreakout BTC 4h barely missed gate (29 trades, 1 short). ETH 4h had 1 trade. The 125-bar squeeze lookback on 4h = 500 hours (20.8 days) before the first squeeze can even be detected. By the time a squeeze-breakout cycle completes, 30-40 days have passed — allowing at most 9-12 cycles per year. With some cycles being false breakouts, the effective trade count drops to 1-29.

**Lesson:** Any strategy with a lookback > 50 bars on 4h will fail the 30-trade gate. Squeeze/consolidation detection is particularly vulnerable because it requires both a minimum lookback for base detection AND additional bars for the breakout to materialize. Use squeeze-based strategies on 1h only.

### 4. The "99% Win Rate Trap" — Confirmed 2nd Instance

Both TrendPullbackRSI (Loop 3) and TSITrend (Loop 23) show 97-99% win rates, Sharpe 12-18, and mathematically impossible equity curves. Common elements: double smoothing + EMA trend filter + crypto trending data. This is now a confirmed anti-pattern — if a strategy reports >95% win rate in backtest, the indicator almost certainly has a look-ahead or smoothing artifact.

**Lesson:** Cap win rate expectations at 55% for crypto trend-following. Anything above 70% is suspicious; above 90% is impossible. Audit the indicator formula for close[t] usage, confirm entry timing is open[t+1], and verify the bias check independently.
