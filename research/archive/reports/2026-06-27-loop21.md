## Anti-Patterns Identified

### Keltner Channel %K on 4h — Normalized ≠ Guaranteed 4h Viability

Keltner Channel %K is a normalized 0-1 threshold indicator (same architecture as BB %B which generates 36-50 4h trades). But KC %K produces only 22 trades on 4h — less than half of BB %B's count. The root cause: KC uses ATR(10) × 2.0 for channel width, which adaptively widens in volatile periods. BB uses Std(20) × 2.0 which is wider in absolute terms but doesn't adapt intra-regime. The adaptive widening reduces the number of channel pierce events — exactly when volatility is high and you'd expect more breakouts, the channel gets wider and prevents them.

- **Lesson:** ATR-based channel width (Keltner, SuperTrend band) is a double-edged sword for 4h: it adapts to volatility (good for signal quality) but reduces signal count (bad for 4h trade scarcity). For 4h normalized threshold strategies, fixed-width channels (BB) generate more trades than adaptive-width channels (KC). If using KC on 4h, lower the multiplier to 1.5 or 1.0 to compensate.
