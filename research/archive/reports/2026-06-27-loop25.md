## New Anti-Patterns

### 2026-06-27 Loop 25: BB Squeeze = Deep Signal Scarcity

**Problem:** BBSqueezeBreakout produced 1-8 trades across ALL 4 combos. This is not a parameter-tuning problem — it's structural. BB squeeze detection (rolling minimum BB width) fires ~0.8% of bars. Price breakout from squeezed bands fires ~0.1% of bars. The joint probability means <10 entries/year even on 1h.

**Root cause:** BB width minimum lookback creates a retrospective condition — the squeeze is only confirmed AFTER 125 bars of being the minimum. By the time squeeze is detected, the breakout may have already occurred or the window has passed. The indicator is designed for visual inspection, not algorithmic entry.

**Lesson:** Bollinger Band squeeze as an entry condition is a structural trade-count killer. Unlike BB %B crossing (which fires 194-196 times/year — Loop 11), the squeeze detection acts as a retrospective gate that eliminates 99% of potential entry bars. If using BB for entry, use %B threshold crossing, not squeeze detection.

### 2026-06-27 Loop 25: TSI's Double Smoothing = ETH Noise Amplifier

**Problem:** TSITrend ETH 1h: 63 trades, Sharpe=-0.17. TSITrend ETH 4h: 13 trades, Sharpe=0.28. TSI's double-EMA smoothing (short=13, long=25) on ETH's noisy microstructure produces signals that are either too late (1h) or too sparse (4h).

**Root cause:** TSI's formula EMA(EMA(Δp, 13), 25) means the final output is the 3rd derivative of price — price → momentum → smoothed momentum → smoothed smoothed momentum. Each integration step adds latency and filters genuine signal. On BTC (lower noise), this works marginally (Sharpe=0.63). On ETH (higher noise), the 3-stage smoothing erases all predictive content.

**Lesson:** TSI belongs to the oscillator family but with DOUBLE smoothing penalty. On 1h, standard oscillators (RSI, Stochastic, CCI) react in ~14 bars; TSI reacts in ~38 bars (13+25). For ETH, prefer single-smoothed oscillators or acceleration-based indicators. TSI's claimed advantage — "sharper turning points" — does not materialize on crypto.
