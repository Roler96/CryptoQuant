## Anti-Patterns Discovered

### Loop 34: Triple Smoothing on 4h — Extreme Trade Scarcity (<15 trades)

TRIX (triple EMA + signal-line EMA = 4 smoothing stages) produced 10-16 trades on 4h — even less than single-EMA (20-35) or double-EMA (15-25) indicators. Each additional smoothing layer further attenuates signal frequency on 4h's limited bar count (2190 bars/year).

**Lesson:** Smoothing depth and 4h trade count are inversely proportional:
- 0 stages (raw price, %B, raw MFI): 50-200 trades (viable)
- 1 stage (single EMA, SMA): 20-35 trades (barely viable)
- 2 stages (double EMA, RMI, MAMA/FAMA): 15-25 trades (borderline/usually fail)
- 3+ stages (TRIX, triple EMA): <15 trades (guaranteed failure)

For 4h strategies, restrict smoothing to ≤1 stage. Triple-smoothed indicators should be 1h-only.

### Loop 34: Triple Smoothing on ETH 1h — Signal Destruction

TRIX BTC 1h Sharpe=1.19 vs TRIX ETH 1h Sharpe=0.26. Both use identical parameters, 2 conditions, and produce similar trade counts (72 vs 66). The difference is purely signal quality: triple smoothing destructively filters ETH's microstructure signals, preserving only noise-correlated crossings.

**Lesson:** Triple-smoothing indicators (TRIX, triple EMA cross, smoothed-of-smoothed oscillators) should not be used on ETH, regardless of timeframe. ETH's microstructure requires lightweight signal processing (≤1 EMA stage). Volume-weighted indicators (MFI, Force Index) are the exception — the volume term preserves signal integrity during smoothing.

### Loop 34: MFI (Volume-Weighted Normalized Oscillator) Survives ETH OOS

MFI ETH 1h (OOS=0.74) joins ForceIndexTrend ETH 1h (Loop 10) as the only strategies to achieve ETH OOS validation. Both are volume-weighted. The common property: volume acts as a signal-strength multiplier that naturally adapts to changing microstructure, unlike price-only oscillators whose signal distribution shifts when the regime changes.

**Lesson:** Volume-weighted normalized oscillators (MFI 0-100) are the most ETH-robust indicator family identified. For ETH deployment, prefer MFI or Force Index over any price-only oscillator. The combination of (a) volume weighting + (b) 0-100 normalization + (c) trend filter appears to be the minimum viable ETH strategy template.
